Painter, draughtsman, father, engineer.  
I have been painting since I was fourteen — first out of passion, then out of habit, now out of pleasure and curiosity.

I built this site myself — with some code, a few modern tools, and a genuine interest in new technologies. It is deliberately minimal and slightly nostalgic: because art needs no embellishment, and because I like things to be seen for what they are.

Here you will find my story: old paintings and new ones, exhibitions, newspaper clippings, experiments. A personal archive open to the world — around **60–70 works** produced since 2002.
